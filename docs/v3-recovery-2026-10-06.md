quarantine into persistent local storage. Ten unique sensor captures passed

## Wheel-power units

The concrete game wheel class exposes power as a 0–1 ratio, whereas its public
interface and terminal expose a percentage. The plugin was logging the ratio
under `wheelPowerPercent`, so V3's 0.15 was misread as 0.15% instead of 15%.
The plugin now reports both the ratio and the corrected percentage. The earlier
power-floor trial labels are withdrawn; they do not support a calibrated
power-versus-progress conclusion. See [actuation evidence](headless-actuation.md)
for the assembly fingerprint and the corrected interpretation.

The correction compiled and loaded in the disposable worker. Run
`170e834d187a6a5d206225ad263ffd2d` contained no movement command, reached readiness
with fresh logs, and stopped cleanly with no target-fatal markers. Its staged
plugin hash was
`791e31a4c8e29e8eca2ed46d86f181bf7c17f780dc059d9bd9bd683f37113b23`.

## Runner lifecycle

The startup probe exposed a brief false pass: the new activation was paired
with the previous run's ready game log before a fresh log existed. VRageCage's
local receipt parser now excludes log evidence older than the activation and
reports log freshness explicitly. Missing or invalid activation timestamps
fail that check. A temporary copy of the patched parser verified the final
live smoke receipt successfully; the installed worker parser was not replaced.

The local CLI also returns failed terminal operations immediately, instead of
waiting out its wall-clock deadline, and the receipt parser now recognizes
`attitude-limit` as terminal. Four focused wait-lifecycle tests and the full
worker script suite pass. The two core suites pass 16/16 and 13/13 checks.
Changes remain uncommitted and unpublished.

## Navigation remains unresolved

The preceding 600-tick drive trial timed out after negligible progress toward
Starting Area. Correcting the power measurement does not fix or prove the
vehicle controller. The next useful trial should isolate actuator direction,
wheel contact, and steering response with the correct units, rather than
reusing the invalid low-power interpretation.
